# Apple Inc. — Strategic Analysis & Investment Assessment

## About the Project
This project is a strategic and competitive analysis of **Apple Inc.**, developed during my Erasmus+ study programt at **Aix-Marseille-Université**.

Written from the perspective of a financial and strategy consultant advising a potential investor, the report aims to answer a clear question: **is Apple a solid company to invest in for the long term?**

## Frameworks Used
To evaluate Apple's business model, external environment, and competitive advantage, the analysis covers:
* **PESTEL Analysis:** Assessing macroeconomic, regulatory, technological, and environmental trends affecting Apple's global operations.
* **Porter's Five Forces:** Evaluating industry rivalry, supplier dependence, customer loyalty, and barriers to entry.
* **SWOT Analysis:** Mapping Apple's internal strengths (brand value, integrated ecosystem, cash generation) against key vulnerabilities and growth opportunities.

## Key Conclusion
Based on the strategic and competitive findings, **the report concludes with a positive investment recommendation**. Despite regulatory scrutiny and supply chain risks, Apple's strong customer retention, high-margin services growth, and resilient ecosystem make it an attractive long-term investment.
